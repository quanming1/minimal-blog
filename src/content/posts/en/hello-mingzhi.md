This is the first post of Mingzhi.

## Why "Mingzhi"

"明" (Ming) comes from the author's given name, and from a famous line in Zhuge Liang's *Admonition to His Son*: **"Without indifference there is no clear aspiration; without tranquility there is no lasting achievement."**

Blogging, at its core, is a form of sedimentation: writing down what you've figured out, and writing out what you haven't. Write plainly, write quietly — no chasing trends, no chasing traffic. That is "Mingzhi."

## What this site covers

- Technical notes: engineering practice, pitfalls, tool exploration
- Reading & thinking: books, articles, things beyond code
- Occasional ramblings: not guaranteed useful, guaranteed honest
