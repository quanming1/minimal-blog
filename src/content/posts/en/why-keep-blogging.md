## Fragments and sediment

Social media slices expression into 140 characters, 30 seconds, a single comment. They are built for spreading, not for thinking.

A 2,000-word essay takes hours to write. In those hours, you must organize your argument into a structure, turn the structure into paragraphs, and polish paragraphs into sentences — **the process itself is the thinking**. When you can't write, it's usually not because you're lazy, but because you haven't thought it through.

## Who is it for

First, for the future self. Reading it back six months later, you'll be amazed at your naivety — and grateful for the record.

Second, for the passerby. The beauty of the internet is that honest writing eventually finds the people who need it — even if it's just ten readers, they are ten real, useful readers.

## How to keep it going

- Don't chase frequency; chase honesty in every post
- Allow drafts to be imperfect; finish first, perfect later
- Treat writing as practice, not performance
